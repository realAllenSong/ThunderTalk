Offline dictation on Mac: the options compared

Updated 2026-11-22

To dictate on a Mac without an internet connection, you need speech recognition that runs on the Mac itself. Four options do this: Apple Dictation (built in, free, offline on Apple Silicon for supported languages), ThunderTalk (free and open source, several local models, strong on mixed Chinese–English), superwhisper (paid, local and cloud models) and MacWhisper (paid, mainly for transcribing files). Cloud apps such as Wispr Flow and Typeless do not work offline.

How to choose

  • You just need occasional dictation in English: start with Apple Dictation. It is already on your Mac.
  • You dictate a lot, care about privacy, or speak Chinese and English together: ThunderTalk. It is free, runs locally by default and lets you pick the model that suits your Mac.
  • You want AI rewriting modes and don't mind paying: superwhisper.
  • You mainly transcribe interviews or recordings: MacWhisper, or ThunderTalk Studio, which also labels speakers and takes YouTube or Bilibili links.

How fast and accurate is it?

ThunderTalk's default dictation model is ThunderTalk ASR 0.6B, its own fine-tune of Qwen3-ASR-0.6B, running in 8-bit on the Apple Silicon GPU. From the project's release notes:

  • Fewer errors: 8.0% token error rate on the standard test set, against 10.1% for the previous default model, and about 2.3× faster. A later version lowered it to 7.66%.
  • Names: on a 724-name test, names written correctly rose from 67.7% to 80.4%. Hotwords let you teach it the names and product terms it would otherwise miss.
  • Long dictations: speech is recognized while you keep talking, so across 36 dictations of 30 seconds to 4.5 minutes the wait after you stop fell from a 3.4 s median to 0.45 s.

What "offline" really means

Apple says Dictation on Apple Silicon Macs runs on the device for supported languages; on Intel Macs, or for languages without on-device support, it still sends what you say to Apple. Local-model apps such as ThunderTalk download their models once, after which dictation and transcription work with no connection.

FAQ

Can I dictate on a Mac without internet?

Yes. Apple Dictation works offline on Apple Silicon Macs for supported languages, and apps with local models, such as ThunderTalk, work fully offline once their model is downloaded.

What is the best free offline dictation app for Mac?

For dictation in any app, ThunderTalk is free, open source and runs locally by default. Apple Dictation is the simplest built-in option.

Does offline dictation work for Chinese?

ThunderTalk handles Chinese, English and mixed sentences offline. Apple Dictation supports Chinese where on-device support is available, but mixing languages within one sentence is harder.
