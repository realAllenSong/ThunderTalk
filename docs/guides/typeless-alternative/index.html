A free, local alternative to Typeless

Updated 2026-11-22

ThunderTalk is a free, open-source alternative to Typeless for the Mac. You press a key in any app and speak, and the text appears at the cursor, like Typeless, but recognition runs on your Mac, there is no weekly word limit, and nothing needs an account. Typeless processes speech in the cloud and its Pro plan costs $30 a month, or $144 billed yearly.

Why people look for a Typeless alternative

  • The free tier shrank. Reports from autumn 2026 say the free weekly allowance was cut, and the pricing page now only says "limited words".
  • Cloud processing. Reviews note that Typeless sends audio to cloud servers for recognition, which matters for confidential meetings and work notes.
  • Price. Unlimited use costs $30 a month, or $144 a year.

How fast and accurate is it?

ThunderTalk's default dictation model is ThunderTalk ASR 0.6B, its own fine-tune of Qwen3-ASR-0.6B, running in 8-bit on the Apple Silicon GPU. From the project's release notes:

  • Fewer errors: 8.0% token error rate on the standard test set, against 10.1% for the previous default model, and about 2.3× faster. A later version lowered it to 7.66%.
  • Names: on a 724-name test, names written correctly rose from 67.7% to 80.4%. Hotwords let you teach it the names and product terms it would otherwise miss.
  • Long dictations: speech is recognized while you keep talking, so across 36 dictations of 30 seconds to 4.5 minutes the wait after you stop fell from a 3.4 s median to 0.45 s.

When Typeless is the better choice

If you mostly want an assistant that rewrites and formats your speech, or you work on Windows today, Typeless may suit you better. ThunderTalk focuses on accurate, private dictation and transcription; it runs on Apple Silicon Macs now, with Windows and Linux versions planned.

FAQ

Is ThunderTalk a good free Typeless alternative?

Yes, if you are on an Apple Silicon Mac. It does the same press-to-talk dictation in any app, with no word limit and no subscription, and it recognizes speech on your Mac.

Does ThunderTalk have a word limit?

No. There is no daily or weekly limit.

Can ThunderTalk clean up or polish my dictation?

Yes, optionally. AI Proofread can use tools you already have, such as Claude Code, Codex or a local model, and it only fixes misrecognized words.

Is my audio uploaded?

No, not by default. Audio is recognized on your Mac.
